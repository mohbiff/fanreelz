Delete your Fanreelz account or data

You can request deletion without installing or opening the app.

Delete your account

  1. Email hello@fanreelz.com from the email address linked to your Fanreelz account.
  2. Use the subject “Delete my Fanreelz account”. If you cannot use that email address, contact us so we can verify that the account belongs to you. Never send your password.
  3. We will handle your request within 30 days, as described in our privacy policy.

Request account deletion

What is deleted

Your sign-in account, profile, and associated ratings, comments, likes, votes, playlists, follows, saved history, watch-later lists, preferences, requests, submissions and private safety records linked to your account are included in the request.

Deleting a Fanreelz account does not delete videos hosted by YouTube, TikTok, Vimeo or other platforms, or your accounts with those platforms.

Data that may remain

Anonymous aggregate statistics that cannot be linked back to you may remain. If any identifiable information needs to be retained for a legal or security reason, we will explain the information and retention period when handling your request.

Delete specific data and keep your account

Email hello@fanreelz.com and tell us which information you want deleted. Use your account email address so we can verify the request.
