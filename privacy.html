Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Fanreelz (fanreelz.com) is a fan site that collects AI-made fan films from other platforms and lets fans rate and discuss them. This page explains what we store about you and why.

What we store

  • When you sign in with Google or Discord: a user ID, your display name, your profile picture and your email address, as provided by Google or Discord. Your name and picture are shown next to your ratings and comments. Your email is never shown to other users.
  • What you do on Fanreelz: your ratings, comments, likes, votes, playlists, follows, requests and the films you submit. Ratings, comments and similar activity are visible to other visitors.
  • Settings: preferences such as the newsletter or family mode, visible only to you.
  • Community safety: your blocked-member choices, and reports you send about content or members. Reports include your account ID, the reported content, your reason and optional details, and our review outcome. Block lists and reporter identities are private; Fanreelz moderators can review reports to enforce the community rules.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts and activity (servers in the EU).
  • Netlify hosts the website.
  • Google and Discord handle sign-in. We never see your password.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views and visits so we know how many people use Fanreelz. It sets no cookies and does not track individual visitors.
  • Umami measures how long visits last and which pages are read, also without cookies and without identifying you.
  • YouTube, TikTok, Vimeo, Instagram and Dailymotion play the films through their own players. When a film plays, that platform may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time, including deleting your account and everything you have posted. Email hello@fanreelz.com and we will handle it within 30 days. If you are in the EU you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in Sweden, IMY).

Contact

Questions about privacy or requests to remove content: hello@fanreelz.com
